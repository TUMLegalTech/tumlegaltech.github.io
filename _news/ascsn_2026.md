Two papers accepted at the upcoming ACM CS&LAW Conference in Berkeley (March 2026): [Niklas Wais](/people/niklas-wais/), [Max Prior](/people/max-prior/), and [Runsheng Chen](/people/runsheng-chen/) will present "AppeaLLM: Mimicking a Closed-World Environment for Court Decision Prediction," and [Matthias Grabmair](/people/matthias-grabmair/) co-authored "Thinking Longer, Not Always Smarter: Evaluating LLM Capabilities in Hierarchical Legal Reasoning" with Li Zhang, Morgan Gray, and Kevin Ashley.
