Two papers from our group were accepted to EMNLP 2026! [Sebastian Nagl](/people/sebastian-nagl/) presents "[BenGER: Benchmarking LLM Systems on Subsumption-Based Legal Reasoning in German Law](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1266/)," co-authored with Ann-Kristin Mayrhofer, Martin Heidebach, Aleyna Koçak, Anne Zettelmeier, [Elly Breu](/people/elly-breu/), [Angelina Greiner](/people/angelina-greiner/), [Sofija Milijas](/people/sofija-milijas/), and [Matthias Grabmair](/people/matthias-grabmair/). [Julius Vernie](/people/julius-vernie/) presents his work "[By Their Fruits You Will Know Them: Comparing Formalizations of Law by the Decisions They Encode](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.2011/)," co-authored with [Matthias Grabmair](/people/matthias-grabmair/).
