Über das Stipendium

Das Stipendium ermöglicht herausragenden Master-Studierenden der TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT), ihre Masterarbeit im Bereich Legal Tech mit einem mehrmonatigen Forschungsaufenthalt am renommierten CodeX – The Stanford Center for Legal Informatics zu verbinden. Ziel der Förderung ist es, exzellente Nachwuchskräfte an der Schnittstelle von Informatik und Recht zu unterstützen und die transatlantische Forschungskooperation zwischen München und Stanford zu stärken.

Die Partner

  • CodeX – The Stanford Center for Legal Informatics ist ein gemeinsames Zentrum der Stanford Law School und des Stanford Department of Computer Science und gilt als weltweit führende Einrichtung an der Schnittstelle von Recht und Technologie. Schwerpunkte sind u. a. Computational Law, Legal Informatics, Legal Analytics und KI im juristischen Kontext.
  • Professur für Legal Tech, TUM (Prof. Dr. Matthias Grabmair) forscht an der TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) zu maschinellem Lernen, Natural Language Processing und deren Anwendung auf rechtliche Fragestellungen.
  • Legal Tech Colab (LTC) von UnternehmerTUM und TUM Venture Labs ist die zentrale Plattform für Legal-Tech-Innovation im Münchner Ökosystem. Das LTC verbindet Wissenschaft, Praxis und Start-ups, fördert den Transfer von Forschungsergebnissen in die Anwendung und unterstützt das Stipendium als Co-Organisator und Brücke zur Legal-Tech-Community.

About the scholarship

The scholarship enables outstanding master's students at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) to combine their master's thesis in Legal Tech with a research stay of several months at the renowned CodeX – The Stanford Center for Legal Informatics. The goal of the funding is to support excellent early-career researchers at the intersection of computer science and law and to strengthen transatlantic research cooperation between Munich and Stanford.

The partners

  • CodeX – The Stanford Center for Legal Informatics is a joint center of Stanford Law School and the Stanford Department of Computer Science and is considered the world's leading institution at the intersection of law and technology. Its focus areas include computational law, legal informatics, legal analytics and AI in legal contexts.
  • Professorship for Legal Tech, TUM (Prof. Dr. Matthias Grabmair) conducts research at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) on machine learning, natural language processing and their application to legal problems.
  • Legal Tech Colab (LTC) of UnternehmerTUM and TUM Venture Labs is the central platform for Legal Tech innovation in the Munich ecosystem. The LTC connects academia, practice and start-ups, promotes the transfer of research results into application, and supports the scholarship as co-organiser and bridge to the Legal Tech community.

Who can apply?

Please use the following checklist to confirm that you meet the requirements:

  • Enrolled in a master's programme at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) at the Technical University of Munich
  • No master's studies abroad – the master's thesis must be written as part of your master's programme at TUM
  • Maximum age 35 years at the time of application
  • A concrete topic proposal in the field of Legal Tech for the master's thesis (1-page exposé), naming at least one thematically suitable CodeX mentor from the list given
  • Very good English language skills, written and spoken, evidenced for example by TOEFL, IELTS or an equivalent certificate
  • Willingness to undertake a research stay of 3 to 6 months at CodeX in Stanford

🌍 Note for international applicants: Applications are open to all students enrolled in one of the CIT master's programmes named above at TUM – regardless of nationality. Applicants are themselves responsible for clarifying the visa requirements for a research stay in the USA in good time.

What the scholarship provides

  • €10,000 lump-sum funding for travel, accommodation and living costs during the stay at Stanford
  • Payment in two instalments:
    1. First instalment at the beginning of the stay
    2. Second instalment upon submission of a short interim report
  • Payment is made exclusively to a German bank account held by the scholarship recipient
  • Affiliation with CodeX Stanford
  • Co-supervision of the master's thesis by the TUM Professorship for Legal Tech
  • Access to the network of CodeX and of the Professorship for Legal Tech (Prof. Grabmair)
  • Access to mentors from the CodeX environment, such as: Prof. Mike Genesereth, Dr. Marzieh Nabi, Prof. Harry Surden, Prof. Oliver Goodenough, Dr. Robert Mahari, Prof. Sandy Pentland, Prof. Thibault Schrepel and Dr. Roland Vogl

💡 The scholarship is designed as lump-sum support. Individual costs beyond this amount are not reimbursed and must be borne by the scholarship recipients themselves.

If the research stay or the master's thesis is discontinued, the scholarship must be repaid on a pro-rata basis.

Selection process & timeline

How the selection works

Scholarship recipients are selected by an internal committee of the Professorship for Legal Tech at the Technical University of Munich and the Legal Tech Colab (LTC), in close coordination with CodeX – The Stanford Center for Legal Informatics.

The assessment considers in particular:

  • academic performance in bachelor's and master's studies
  • quality, originality and feasibility of the submitted topic exposé
  • motivation and fit with the research focus of CodeX
  • suitability for a research stay of several months at Stanford

The committee's decision is final; there is no legal entitlement to the scholarship.

FAQ

Can I apply if I am doing my master's degree abroad?

No. Active enrolment in a master's programme at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) at the Technical University of Munich is a prerequisite. Master's studies abroad are not eligible for funding.

Does the topic of the master's thesis have to be fixed at the time of application?

Yes. A one-page topic exposé in the field of Legal Tech must be submitted with the application. Adjustments and refinements in coordination with the supervisors in Munich and Stanford are of course possible at a later stage.

How long is the stay at Stanford?

The stay at CodeX is flexibly designed for 3 to 6 months and is arranged individually with the Professorship for Legal Tech (Prof. Grabmair) and CodeX Stanford.

How is the scholarship paid out?

The €10,000 is paid in two instalments: a first instalment at the beginning of the stay at Stanford and a second instalment after submission of a short interim report.

Who decides on the award?

An internal committee of the Professorship for Legal Tech (Prof. Grabmair) and the Legal Tech Colab (LTC) at TUM, in coordination with CodeX Stanford. There is no legal entitlement to the scholarship.

Can I apply even though I do not hold German citizenship?

Yes. The scholarship is open to all students enrolled in a master's programme at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT) at the Technical University of Munich – regardless of nationality. Applicants are themselves responsible for clarifying the visa requirements for the USA.

Can I combine the scholarship with other funding?

Combining it with other scholarships is generally possible. Please state in your application whether and for which other scholarships you are applying in parallel or are already receiving funding.

📄 Note on the source of funds
The funds originate from the project „Legal Tech Colab“, approved by the Bavarian State Ministry of Justice (funding reference: B 1 - 5120E - VI - 5116/2022), in the 2026 budget year. The funding may be used exclusively for project-related expenses (e.g. travel, accommodation, study costs). The scholarship recipient undertakes to submit confirmation of completion together with a simple proof of use (a tabular overview of expenses) no later than four weeks after the end of the funding period.
