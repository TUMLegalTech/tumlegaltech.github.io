Runsheng Chen is a Ph.D. student in the TUM Legal Tech Group at the Technical University of Munich. Before that, he received his M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence from TUM and his B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from Southeast University, China.

His research interests include (Legal) Information Retrieval for Large Language Models, Large Reasoning Models, and Model Interpretability. He is particularly interested in developing methods to enhance the reasoning capabilities of large language models and making their decision-making processes more transparent and interpretable.
